Kontur UI and Kontur.Guides

Kontur's design system. Every product in the company is built on it — about 100 commercial and internal services. I develop, maintain and roll it out, and own its website, Kontur.Guides.

What it is

  • ⚡️ Kontur UI — shared components and rules for Kontur designers and developers. The component library for developers is open source on GitHub
  • 📚 Kontur.Guides — the design system website: principles, rules and components in one place

My role

  • 🫰 I lead the infrastructure area: 11 designers working on the design system, widgets and shared services such as the customer account
  • 🛠 Develop the system and help product teams adopt it
  • 📚 Own Kontur.Guides

Impact

  • 🧩 The design system is used across all Kontur products — about 100 services
  • 👥 About 2,000 people use Kontur.Guides regularly, including outside Kontur
  • 🌍 Designers at large companies in Russia, the CIS and Europe rely on our principles
