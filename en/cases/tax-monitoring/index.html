Tax Monitoring

Kontur's service for tax monitoring. I designed its interface from scratch — what it would be and how it would scale as the product grew.

What it is

Tax monitoring is a way for large companies in Russia to work with the tax authority: instead of on-site audits, the company gives the Federal Tax Service online access to its data and reports. Kontur's service helps companies run this exchange.

My role

I was the product's designer and designed the interface from scratch.

Impact

Sales results and customer feedback showed the interface was one of the reasons clients chose Kontur. It helped the product reach 2nd place in an already crowded market.
